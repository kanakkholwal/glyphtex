\section{From the teaching lab}
The first year lab reopened in September with 40 new fume hoods and digital balances at every bench.
Students now record results on tablets, which cuts marking time and lets demonstrators spot mistakes during the session.

Thanks to the technical team, who rebuilt the lab over the summer while keeping outreach sessions running.
If you would like to help design new second year experiments, contact the teaching coordinator.
