\begin{poem}{Kettle}
Before the light, the kettle starts its low \\
and patient argument with cold. \\
I stand and let it win.

Replace these lines with your own poem. \\
A blank line starts a new stanza, \\
and the last line needs no backslashes.
\end{poem}

\begin{poem}{Inventory of a Small Window}
One sparrow, one wire, \\
a neighbour's shirt that will not dry, \\
the whole of Tuesday.
\end{poem}
